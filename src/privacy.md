CapeX Transport Group (Pty) Ltd (Reg 2024/702806/07), Cape Town. Last updated <span class="placeholder">[date]</span>. This policy explains how we handle personal information under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

## What we collect

Names, phone numbers and email addresses; pick-up and drop-off addresses; trip bookings and history; boarding confirmations and trip GPS data; and, for drivers and vehicle owners, the identity, licence, vehicle and banking documents we need to contract with them.

When you fill in a form on this website we collect your name, email, mobile number, company and the details you add (for example headcount, shift times or an event date).

## Why we use it

To plan and run trips, confirm who boarded, keep riders and drivers safe, invoice clients, pay drivers and owners, reply to enquiries, and meet our legal obligations. If you request a quote or download a guide, we may follow up once by email.

## Who we share it with

Only the people who need it to deliver your trip (your driver sees your name, pick-up point and code), your employer if they book transport for you, and service providers who host our systems and send our emails. We never sell personal information.

## How long we keep it

For as long as you use our services and then for the period the law requires for financial and tax records. <span class="placeholder">[Retention periods: confirm.]</span>

## Analytics and spam protection

We use Google Analytics to understand which pages are useful, and Google reCAPTCHA to block spam on forms.
